# What's Next: A Text-Based Horror Adventure Game

Welcome to **What's Next**, a thrilling text-based horror adventure game that will test your decision-making and bravery. Traverse through eerie rooms, inspect unsettling objects, and uncover the dark secrets lurking within. Choose your actions wisely, as they determine your fate.

## Features
- Immersive horror narrative with multiple endings
- Dynamic room and clue selection with consequences
- Inventory management to collect and use key items
- Atmospheric storytelling with vivid descriptions

## Requirements
- Python 3.7 or higher

## How to Play
1. Clone or download the repository.
2. Run the game using the command:
    ```bash
    python text_horror_game.py
    ```
3. Follow the on-screen instructions to make decisions.
4. Choose rooms and inspect clues to progress through the story.
5. Keep track of your inventory, as certain items may determine your survival.

## Game Mechanics
- **Room Exploration:** Choose to explore the Parlor, Basement, Kitchen, or Bedroom.
- **Clue Inspection:** Investigate eerie objects to reveal hidden secrets.
- **Inventory Management:** Some items may aid your progress, while others may not.
- **Multiple Endings:** Your choices impact the story's outcome.

## Controls
- Type `y` to confirm actions and `n` to decline.
- Select room or clue options using their corresponding numbers.
- Type `random` for a randomly chosen option.

## Example Gameplay
- "Should I inspect the old photograph? [y or n]"
- "Choose a room: 1) Parlor 2) Basement 3) Kitchen 4) Bedroom"
- "Continue? [y or n]"

## Tips
- Pay attention to narrative details, as they may provide clues.
- Items in your inventory can change the outcome of the story.
- Think carefully before making choices — your life depends on it.

## Known Issues
- Ensure input is lowercase and properly formatted (`y`, `n`, or numbers).
- Unexpected inputs may result in error messages.
